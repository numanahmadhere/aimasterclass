## Cold
When lows fall below 5 C, advise warm clothing and care for people without heating. Below 0 C, warn about frost on roads.
 
## Rain
Heavy rain over 50 mm in a day can flood low areas. Advise avoiding underpasses and keeping phones charged.
 
## Alert rules
Send an alert only for heat, wind, cold or rain conditions that cross the thresholds in these guidelines. Do not repeat an alert about the same condition on consecutive days.
